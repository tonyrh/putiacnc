The Hershey single-stroke fonts (futural, futuram, rowmans, rowmand, scripts, cursive)

The Hershey Fonts were originally created by Dr. A. V. Hershey while working at the U. S. National Bureau of Standards.

The format of the font data in this distribution was originally created by James Hurt, Cognition, Inc., 900 Technology Park Drive,
Billerica, MA 01821.

The fonts may be used by anyone for any purpose, commercial or otherwise, provided these acknowledgements are distributed with the
font data. They may be converted into any other format except the one distributed by the U.S. NTIS; the .hf files here are in a
format of their own (see scripts/gen_hershey.py), made from the public-domain data as packaged in the Hershey-Fonts Python package.
